# Research notes

Godavari Ferry is fictional. These notes fix the place, the gap and the licenses. Eight sources, three competitors, under the §16.0 caps.

| # | Area | Source URL | Takeaway | Feeds § |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Place | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dowleswaram_Barrage | The barrage at Dowleswaram (Sir Arthur Cotton) is the downstream crossing at Rajahmundry / Rajamahendravaram. The mark is its piers, not a wave or a letter. | §2, §3 |
| 2 | Place | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godavari | The Godavari is the river. The brand name is the river, not a coined startup word. | §1 |
| 3 | Market | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajahmundry | Rajamahendravaram is the city. Country boats and seasonal rides are local; there is no single published passenger timetable brand to copy. | §1 |
