# Brief — Godavari Ferry

Godavari Ferry Co. is a fictional operator. It is not a real company and it does not run the boats at Rajamahendravaram. The place is real: the passenger crossing between Gowthami Ghat (also spelled Gautami) and the steps at the Dowleswaram Barrage, the Sir Arthur Cotton barrage, on the Godavari.

1. The business. A posted timetable and a paper ticket for that crossing. Passengers speak Telugu first, then English, with Hindi for people coming off the trains. The owner of the system is the jetty office.
2. The job. A timetable site and the jetty board (digital product), plus the paper ticket and pier cards (print). Marketing and motion are switched off: there is no campaign and no film.
3. Personality. Plain, punctual, river-literate. It is not a luxury cruise, not a startup, and not a tourist poster.
4. What exists. No prior logo, no colour file, no component library of our own. The template components (Button, Input, Card, Badge) are reused. Local country boats publish no timetable. APTDC runs pleasure boats, which are a different product. The bus over the bridge is the other way across.
5. References. A paper ticket, the barrage piers, a departure board, Atkinson Hyperlegible, a hull number in red oxide.
6. Constraints. Fonts must be SIL OFL and shipped as files. Contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark. No paid icon set: Lucide, outline. High contrast is not a separate theme in this version.

Played back: a fictional ferry posts the next boat between Gowthami Ghat and Dowleswaram, on a ticket you can read in full sun.
