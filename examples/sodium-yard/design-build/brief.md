# Brief — Sodium Yard

Sodium Yard is fictional. It is not a real contractor and it does not run the street lamps in Visakhapatnam. The place is real: the municipal night shift that logs dead lamps and sends a van.

1. The business. A night desk that names the next pole, and a morning handover sheet. Crew speak Telugu first, then English, with Hindi for people who came in from another ward. The owner of the system is the night desk.
2. The job. The desk and the van list (digital product), plus the handover sheet and pole cards (print). Marketing and motion are switched off: there is no campaign and no film.
3. Personality. Terse, nocturnal, exact. It is not a startup, not a sci-fi HUD, and not a lifestyle brand.
4. What exists. No prior logo, no colour file, no component library of our own. The template components (Button, Input, Card, Badge) are reused. The electricity-board paper slip does not name the pole. A private electrician who does not log the pole is a different product. A street that stays dark until morning is the failure this desk exists to stop.
5. References. A lamp head on a pole, sodium amber on a blue-black yard, a work order, Public Sans, a filament used as a rule.
6. Constraints. Fonts must be SIL OFL and shipped as files. Contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA in dark and light. No paid icon set: Lucide, outline. High contrast is not a separate theme in this version. Dark is the default theme.

Played back: a fictional night desk names the next dead lamp in Visakhapatnam, and the morning sheet is the same log in daylight.
