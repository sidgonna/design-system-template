# Research notes

Sodium Yard is fictional. These notes fix the place, the gap and the licenses. Eight sources, three competitors, under the §16.0 caps.

| # | Area | Source URL | Takeaway | Feeds § |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Place | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visakhapatnam | The yard is in Visakhapatnam. The brand names a desk in that city, not a coined product. | §1 |
| 2 | Place | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium-vapor_lamp | A sodium lamp is amber. The primary ramp is that colour, flat ink, not a glow. | §2, §4 |
| 3 | Market | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_light | Municipal street lighting is logged by pole, not by a campaign. There is no single published night-desk brand to copy. | §1 |
| 4 | Competitor | https://www.apeasternpower.com/ | The electricity-board complaint slip is paper and does not name the next van. We do not use their greens or their form tone. | §1, §2 |
| 5 | Competitor | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrician | A private electrician who does not log the pole is a different job. The desk exists so the pole number survives the shift. | §1 |
| 6 | Accessibility | https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ | Body text 4.5:1, large text and UI 3:1. The night desk is the default theme, so the base values are the dark ones. | §4, §11 |
| 7 | Type | https://github.com/ateliertriay/bricolage | Bricolage Grotesque is SIL OFL. Used for the lamp state. Public Sans (also OFL) is the shipped fallback and the body. | §5 |
